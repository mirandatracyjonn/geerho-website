News

Updates and announcements from Geerho.

Listing a home now needs a plan

October 16, 2026

To list a home for sale or for rent on Geerho, you now need a Basic plan (or Pro or Elite). Geerho never takes a cut of a home sale or rent, so the plan is how listing homes is paid for.

Don't have a plan yet? When you post a home, we'll ask first and save it as a draft, so nothing is lost: choose a plan, then publish it from Profile → My Listings. Free members can keep posting items as before, and your plan continues until you cancel it. On Basic, you can make 3 new posts a day, like every Basic member. See Plans.

Unlimited Fridays

October 16, 2026

Every Friday, everyone on Geerho can post as much as they like, with or without a plan, and those posts don't count toward your daily or monthly limits. It's Friday in your own time: Philippine time if you live in the Philippines, US Pacific time otherwise. Profile → Plan shows when it's on.

Also new: real estate agents, brokers, and property managers need a Basic, Pro, or Elite plan to add their real estate license and list homes. See Plans.

Geerho moderators can now work from a computer

October 16, 2026

Geerho's moderators can now answer Geerho Support and review reports, licenses, address changes, TikTok level transfers, payments on hold, and account recovery requests on geerho.com as well as in the apps, so your requests get looked at sooner.

Nothing changes about who sees what: only Geerho moderators can open these tools, they see the same information as in the apps, and every decision is logged.

Two-step verification and App Lock

October 15, 2026

You can now add extra protection to your Geerho account, on the iPhone and Android apps and on geerho.com:

  • Two-step verification: go to Profile → Settings → Security → Turn On and scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, and others). Signing in then asks for the 6-digit code from the app.
  • Recovery codes: you get 10 one-time codes to keep somewhere safe, in case you lose your phone.
  • App Lock: in the apps, have Geerho ask for Face ID, Touch ID, or your fingerprint each time you open it. Your face or fingerprint never leaves your phone.

See Help for step-by-step instructions.

geerho.com now works like the app

October 14, 2026

If you use the Geerho app, the website should now feel familiar:

  • The same five tabs: Home, Search, Post, Inbox, and Profile. On a phone they're at the bottom, like the app; on a computer they're at the top.
  • Your Profile, just like the app: My Bids (with Complete Purchase when you win), Purchases, Sales, Purchase Requests, Rentals, Home Tours, Jobs and Quotes, Watching, Favorites, Get Paid, and more. Settings is behind the gear.
  • Buy and bid on the website, with a bigger bid box, quick bids, and a red timer in an auction's last 2 minutes.
  • Search for people as well as listings, and see member profiles.
  • Post from the website: Post → choose what you're listing, add photos, and publish, with the same form as the app. Use My Location or type a city or ZIP code to set the area. Edit your listings from Profile → My Listings.
  • Square, clean photos: when you add a photo, drag it to center your item, then Use Photo. Listing photos now fill their square everywhere.
  • Everything else, too: rent a tool, request a home tour or a quote, respond to a job, post for your Elite team, add a professional license, and change your home address, all on the website.

The Geerho public beta is open

October 10, 2026

Anyone with an iPhone can now try Geerho before it reaches the App Store. Join the beta on TestFlight (Apple's free beta app), then install Geerho. It's free.

New in this version:

  • Instant notifications. Messages arrive within seconds, even when Geerho is closed, with Geerho's own tones, or upload your own.
  • Follow and Contacts. Follow anyone; when you follow each other, you're Contacts.
  • Exact pins. Search your address or use your location to pin the exact meeting spot. Only your buyer, renter, or hired pro sees it.

One account per person, and checkout that fills itself in

October 7, 2026

Every Geerho member is a verified real person, and now each person has exactly one account:

  • Sign in your way. Link Apple, Google, and GitHub to the same account in Settings → Sign-in Methods (or on your Account page here). Sign in with Apple now works on geerho.com too.
  • No duplicate accounts. If someone tries to verify a second account with an ID that's already verified, we don't verify it. We tell them which account is theirs, and we let the original account know, in case someone else is using their ID.
  • Your home address, kept private. If your ID shows your address, it's saved to your account when you verify, so checkout fills it in for you. It's never on your profile. Moved? Send your new address with 2 or 3 recent documents, like a utility bill and a bank statement. A Geerho moderator checks them, and then they're deleted.

We keep as little as we can: never your ID images, just your legal name, date of birth, and a scrambled code made from your ID number that can't be turned back into it. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

Homes, local pros, and jobs are coming to Geerho

October 6, 2026

The next Geerho beta update adds three things our community asked for, starting in California and the Philippines:

  • Homes for rent and for sale. Browse homes with photos, rent or price, bedrooms, and more, then message the lister or request a tour. Rentals show only the neighborhood until a tour is confirmed, which helps stop rental scams.
  • Hire local pros. Freelancers can offer their services, from house cleaning and photography to electrical work, and you can request a quote.
  • Post a job. Need a plumber on Saturday? Post the job and verified freelancers respond with a message and a price.

Real estate agents and people in licensed trades add their license in the app. A Geerho moderator checks it against the issuer’s public records, such as the California DRE or the Philippine PRC, before their profile shows a Licensed badge.
