A Business plan, safer big purchases, and search alerts

October 24, 2026

  • New Business plan: $99.99 a month (₱5,999 in the Philippines). 20 new posts a day, no commission, and a team of up to 10 people who post and answer chats for you. Made for small shops, agencies, and dealers. See Plans.
  • Big purchases: Geerho takes in-app payments up to $2,500 (₱150,000). For anything above that, like a car or a motorcycle, checkout says Request to Buy and you pay the seller directly once they accept. Geerho doesn't hold that money, so its buyer protection doesn't cover it: meet in person, check the item and its papers, and use a bank transfer or escrow you trust. Listing above $2,500 needs a plan.
  • Search alerts: search for something that isn't there yet and tap Alert Me. We'll tell you when one is posted near you. Manage them in Settings.

Our Terms, Return & Refund Policy, and FAQ are updated to match.

Safer chats and quicker answers from Support

October 23, 2026

We've added a few careful AI helpers to keep Geerho safe and friendly. People still make every decision about your account and your listings.

  • Safety tips in chats: if a message looks like a common scam (paying outside Geerho, gift cards, crypto, a deposit before you've seen the item, a suspicious link, or a request for a code), Geerho adds a short safety tip to that chat, at most once a day. It never accuses anyone, and ordinary messages are never checked.
  • Quicker Support answers: everyday questions to Support (general, buying, selling, renting, shipping, something not working, feedback) often get a first answer within a minute or two from the Geerho Assistant. It's an AI, says so, and answers from our Help and FAQ. If it doesn't solve your problem, just reply and a person takes over. Questions about your account, verification, payments, safety, reports, privacy or legal matters always go to a person.
  • Faster reviews: our moderators now get an AI note when a new listing may break our rules, and a short summary of each report, so they can look at urgent things first. Nothing is removed automatically.

The AI is Claude, from Anthropic, which doesn't use this content to train its models. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what is checked.

Getting paid in the US: tax information up front

October 21, 2026

When you set up payouts in Profile → Get Paid, you now choose Personal (your Social Security number) or Business (your company's EIN). Stripe, our payment processor, keeps the number; Geerho never sees it. Payouts wait until it's on file.

Two-step verification and App Lock

October 15, 2026

You can now add extra protection to your Geerho account, on the iPhone and Android apps and on geerho.com:

  • Two-step verification: go to Profile → Settings → Security → Turn On and scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, and others). Signing in then asks for the 6-digit code from the app.
  • Recovery codes: you get 10 one-time codes to keep somewhere safe, in case you lose your phone.
  • App Lock: in the apps, have Geerho ask for Face ID, Touch ID, or your fingerprint each time you open it. Your face or fingerprint never leaves your phone.

See Help for step-by-step instructions.

One account per person, and checkout that fills itself in

October 7, 2026

Every Geerho member is a verified real person, and now each person has exactly one account:

  • Sign in your way. Link Apple, Google, and GitHub to the same account in Settings → Sign-in Methods (or on your Account page here). Sign in with Apple now works on geerho.com too.
  • No duplicate accounts. If someone tries to verify a second account with an ID that's already verified, we don't verify it. We tell them which account is theirs, and we let the original account know, in case someone else is using their ID.
  • Your home address, kept private. If your ID shows your address, it's saved to your account when you verify, so checkout fills it in for you. It's never on your profile. Moved? Send your new address with 2 or 3 recent documents, like a utility bill and a bank statement. A Geerho moderator checks them, and then they're deleted.

We keep as little as we can: never your ID images, just your legal name, date of birth, and a scrambled code made from your ID number that can't be turned back into it. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.
