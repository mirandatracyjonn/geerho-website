Get Geerho for iPhone

Buy, bid, rent, and sell in the app, where every member verifies their identity. You can also message, contact support, and verify on the website.

Coming soon. Our public beta on TestFlight opens shortly, followed by the App Store. Check back here for the download link.

How to get started

  1. Download Geerho on your iPhone (iOS 17 or later).
  2. Sign in and choose a username.
  3. Verify your identity with a government ID and a quick selfie. It takes a few minutes, and our partner Didit checks it, not us.
  4. Buy, bid, rent, sell, and message with other verified members.

Prefer a computer?

Use the same account on geerho.com: sign in, verify your identity, message sellers, and contact support. Buying, bidding, renting, and selling on the website are coming soon. Wherever you use Geerho, every deal keeps a record, with listing IDs, condition photos for rentals, and reviews, so our support team can see what happened if something goes wrong.

Joining the beta

Beta testers use Apple's free TestFlight app. When the beta opens, tap the beta link on this page from your iPhone, install TestFlight if asked, and then install Geerho. Beta testers sign up and verify their identity just like everyone else.
