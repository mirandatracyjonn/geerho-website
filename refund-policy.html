Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains returns and refunds for payments made through Geerho, operated by Geerho Tech LLC (“Geerho,” “we”). It summarises the rules in our Terms of Service (sections 7, 7A, 12 and 13); if the two ever differ, the Terms apply. Nothing here limits the rights you have under the law where you live.

1. Geerho is a marketplace

Items, tool rentals and services on Geerho are offered by members, not by Geerho. Geerho Tech LLC doesn’t sell on Geerho. Returns of an item are between the buyer and the seller: ask the seller in Messages, and check the listing for its return terms. Where you paid through Geerho, Geerho holds your payment until the deal is done, so problems can be fixed before the seller is paid (below).

2. Items bought through Geerho

  • Before the seller accepts: you can cancel a Buy Now request. In the Philippines, where you pay at checkout, Geerho refunds you in full if the seller declines, cancels or doesn’t answer in time.
  • After you pay: Geerho holds the payment until you confirm you received the item, or until three (3) days after a pickup sale or seven (7) days after tracking is added if you report no problem.
  • If something is wrong (not received, not as described): report a problem on the purchase before the payment is released. The payment stays held while Geerho reviews it, and is then refunded to you or paid to the seller.

3. Tool and equipment rentals

  • You’re charged only if the owner accepts your request.
  • If a rental is cancelled after it’s accepted and before pickup, you’re refunded in full.
  • Your security deposit comes back when the return is confirmed, less any late fees under the rental terms (Terms section 7.13).

4. Services and jobs

When you pay a freelancer through Geerho, we hold the payment until you confirm the work is done, or three (3) days after the freelancer marks the job done if you report no problem. A reported problem holds the payment while Geerho reviews it, and the freelancer may refund you at any time before payout.

5. Payments made outside Geerho

If you paid the other member directly (cash, bank transfer or another app), Geerho never held the money and can’t refund it. Settle it with the other member, and report any member who breaks the rules.

6. Plans and featured listings

  • Plans (Basic, Pro, Elite) renew monthly until you cancel on geerho.com. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you paid for; fees already paid aren’t refunded, except where the law requires.
  • Featured listings are a one-time payment. Fees already paid aren’t refunded, except where the law requires or where Geerho removed the listing by mistake.
  • Plans and featuring are sold on geerho.com, not as App Store or Google Play purchases.

7. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, through our payment processors (Stripe in the United States, PayMongo in the Philippines). How long it takes to show depends on your bank, card or e-wallet. If a payment that used an Invite Friends commission-free credit is refunded, the credit is restored.

8. Contact

Include the transaction ID (for example “S-7K3P-9QXM”) when you contact us about a payment.
