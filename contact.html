Contact us

Geerho Support is in the app, so we can see the transaction you’re asking about and reply where you’ll see it.

How to reach Geerho Support

  1. Open the Geerho app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Contact Support. You can also tap the life-preserver button in your Inbox, or Get Help with This Order on a purchase.
  3. Choose the reason, and the transaction ID if it’s about a listing, purchase, or sale.
  4. Describe what happened. You can attach photos, screenshots, a voice message, a YouTube or other video link, or a PDF.

We reply in your Inbox under “Geerho Support,” usually within 1–2 business days. Many questions are answered in the FAQ.

If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, or delete your personal information, choose the reason “Privacy request” in Contact Support, or write to us at the mailing address below. California residents receive a response within 45 days; users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland within one month. See our Privacy Policy.
