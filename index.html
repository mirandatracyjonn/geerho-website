    What you can do

    • Buy and sell

      List new and used items with photos and a price, and choose local pickup, shipping, or both.

    • Auctions

      Let buyers bid, with an optional reserve and Buy It Now price. Watch an item to get an alert before it ends.

    • Rent tools and equipment

      Borrow from people nearby instead of buying. Owners set the rate, deposit, late fee, and how far they'll lend.

    • Homes for rent and sale

      Browse homes with photos and details, message the lister, and request a tour. Rental addresses are shared once a tour is confirmed.

    • Hire local pros

      Find freelancers for cleaning, repairs, photos, lessons, and more, or post a job and get responses. Licensed work needs a checked license.

    • Message in the app

      Chat without sharing your phone number or email. Report or block anyone at any time.

    • Verified members

      Members verify a government ID with a quick selfie check. Buyers, sellers, and renters review each other after every deal.

      More ways to connect with your community.
