    What you can do

    • Buy and sell

      List new and used items with photos and a price, and choose local pickup, shipping, or both.

    • Auctions

      Let buyers bid, with an optional reserve and Buy It Now price. Watch an item to get an alert before it ends.

    • Rent tools and equipment

      Borrow from people nearby instead of buying. Owners set the rate, deposit, late fee, and how far they'll lend, and both sides take condition photos at pickup and return.

    • Message in the app

      Chat without sharing your phone number or email. Report or block anyone at any time.

    • Verified members

      Members verify a government ID with a quick selfie check and earn a Verified badge. Buyers, sellers, and renters review each other after every deal.

    • Coming next

      Homes for rent, lease, and sale, and more ways to connect with your community.

    Ready to buy, bid, or rent?
