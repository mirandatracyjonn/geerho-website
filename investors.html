Investors

Geerho is building a local marketplace where every member is verified, starting in California.

The opportunity

People buy, sell, and rent locally every day, but most local marketplaces can't tell you who's real. Geerho puts identity verification at the center, then brings items for sale, auctions, tool rentals, and soon homes for rent, lease, and sale into one app.

Where we are

  • The iPhone app is built and in testing, with buying and selling, auctions, tool rentals, in-app messaging, reviews, identity verification, and moderation tools.
  • A public beta on Apple's TestFlight is coming soon, launching first in California.
  • Next on the roadmap: property rentals, real estate, and community features.
