Partners

Geerho is building a local marketplace where every member is verified, starting in California. We'd love to work with people who share that goal.

Who we partner with

  • Local businesses and rental shops that want to reach verified neighbors.
  • Property managers, landlords, and licensed real estate professionals, as homes for rent, lease, and sale come to Geerho.
  • Community organizations that help people buy, sell, and share safely.
  • Investors interested in hearing about Geerho's progress.

Where we are

  • The iPhone app is built and in testing, with buying and selling, auctions, tool rentals, in-app messaging, reviews, identity verification, and moderation tools.
  • Members can also browse, sign in, verify, message, and contact support here on geerho.com.
  • A public beta on Apple's TestFlight is coming soon, launching first in California.
  • Next on the roadmap: property rentals, real estate, and community features.
