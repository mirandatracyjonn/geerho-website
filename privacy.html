Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Geerho, LLC (“Geerho,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Geerho mobile app and the website at which this policy is posted (together, the “Services”): a marketplace for buying and selling new and used items, listing property for rent, lease, or sale, and connecting with other people. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the rights and choices you have, wherever you live.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, GitHub, or Apple, we receive your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique account identifier from that provider. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email,” we receive a private relay address instead of your email.
  • Profile information. Your username, display name, profile photo, bio, and city.
  • Listings. Titles, descriptions, prices, categories, condition, photos, and the approximate location of items you list, and the address or approximate location of property you list.
  • Social content. Posts, comments, follows, and other content you share.
  • Messages. Messages you send to and receive from other users through the Services.
  • Purchases and shipping addresses. When you use Buy Now, we store your purchase request, the delivery method, an optional note, and, for shipped items, the shipping address and phone number you enter. If you choose “Save on this iPhone,” the address is also kept in your device's Keychain, not on our servers.
  • Reviews. Star ratings, written reviews, and replies about buyers and sellers after a sale.
  • Identity verification. To sell or send messages, you verify your identity with our verification provider, Didit. Didit collects images of your government ID, a selfie or short video, and the details on your ID (such as your name, date of birth, and document number), and compares your face with your ID photo. Geerho receives only the result (for example, verified or not verified) and the date. We do not receive or store your ID images or face data.
  • Reports and support requests. Information you give us when you report content or contact Geerho Support in the app: the reason, your message, the transaction you choose (its transaction ID and title), and any photos, screenshots, voice recordings, files, or links you attach. Voice recordings are made only when you tap record.
  • Rentals. Rental requests and bookings (dates, rates, deposit, status, and your device’s time zone, used for due dates and reminders) and the condition photos you or the other person take at pickup and return. These are visible only to the owner, the renter, and Geerho’s moderators, and are deleted when either account is deleted. When you request a rental, your approximate area is checked against the owner’s lending radius; it is not saved with the request.
  • Watched items. The listings you choose to watch, and alerts about them. Sellers and other users see only how many people are watching, never who.
  • Recently viewed. The app keeps a list of listings you opened recently on your iPhone only, to show them on the Home screen. It is never sent to Geerho; clear it anytime from Home.
  • Bids. The auctions you bid on, your maximum bids, and when you bid. Other users see bid amounts with your username masked (for example, “t***m”); your maximum bid stays private unless you are outbid.
  • Fee plan. Whether you are a Founding Seller (and your founding number) or otherwise exempt from fees.

Information collected automatically

  • Location. If you allow it, your device’s location while you use the app, to show nearby listings. If you don’t, you can enter a ZIP or postal code instead. Location attached to item listings is coarsened before it is saved, and we remove location data embedded in photos before upload.
  • Device and usage information. Device model, operating system, app version, language, diagnostic and crash data, and how you use features of the Services.
  • Network information. IP address, collected by our hosting providers when you use the Services.
  • Last active time. The date and time you last opened the app while signed in, used to count how many people use Geerho. We do not record which listings or pages you view.

Our Services do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. How we share information

  • With other users. Your public profile, listings, and posts are visible to other users and, for active listings, to visitors who are not signed in. Messages are visible to the people in that conversation. Support requests and their attachments are visible only to you and Geerho’s moderators. When you request to buy an item, your shipping address and phone number (if any) are shared only with that item's seller. Reviews you write are published on the other person's profile with your username, once they review you too or 14 days pass.
  • With service providers who process information on our behalf under contract, including Supabase, Inc. (database, authentication, and file storage), GitHub, Inc. (website hosting), Didit (identity verification), and the sign-in provider you choose (Google LLC, GitHub, Inc., or Apple Inc.).
  • For legal reasons, when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or Geerho.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.
  • With your direction or consent.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or allow third parties to track you across apps and websites.

5. Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account).
  • Edit or delete your profile, listings, and posts in the app.
  • Location: turn device location on or off in iOS Settings.
  • Block or report other users from their profile or a conversation.
  • Privacy requests: contact Geerho Support in the app and choose the reason “Privacy request,” or write to us at the address below.

7. Other U.S. state privacy rights

Residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws, including Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Oregon, Texas, Utah, and Virginia, may have rights to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of their personal information, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising, and certain profiling. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. To make a request, use the methods in section 6. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject “Appeal”; if your appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general.

8. EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Geerho, LLC is the controller of your personal information. Under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, you have the right to:

  • access your personal information and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • erase your information;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We respond to requests within one month, which may be extended by two further months for complex requests. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Other countries

Depending on where you live, including Canada, Brazil, Australia, Japan, and other countries with data protection laws, you may have similar rights to access, correct, and delete your personal information and to complain to a regulator. Contact us to exercise them; we will respond as your local law requires.

10. International data transfers

Geerho is operated from the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. When we transfer personal information from the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland to countries that have not been found to provide adequate protection, we use safeguards approved by those jurisdictions, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

11. Children

The Services are only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We protect personal information with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections, database access rules that limit each user to their own private data, and storage of sign-in credentials in your device’s secure Keychain. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new “Last updated” date, and if the changes are material, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
