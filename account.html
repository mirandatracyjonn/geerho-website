Choose a username

It's how other people find and recognize you on Geerho, on the web and in the app.

Used Geerho before?

Each person can have one Geerho account. If you already signed up another way (for example with Google), don't make a second one: remove this new, empty account, sign in the way you did before, then add this sign-in under Profile → Settings → Sign-in Methods.

You chose Apple's “Hide My Email,” so we couldn't match this Apple ID to your other account automatically.

Lost access to your account?

Can't sign in the way you used to anymore? Geerho moderators can give you your account back after checking that it's really you: a photo of your government ID, two proofs of address, and a short WhatsApp video call where you sign your name three times. This works only for accounts that completed ID verification.

Only Geerho moderators see your documents, and they're deleted when your request ends. The account's owner is told about your request; if it isn't you, they can stop it.
